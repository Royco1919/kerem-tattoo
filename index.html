About

I'm Kerem, a tattoo artist based in Haifa. I work in micro realism: small, precise pieces, mostly portraits of pets, personal objects and quiet moments.

Everything happens at Kerem Studio, a calm and clean space. Each tattoo is planned together with you, from the first idea to the stencil.
