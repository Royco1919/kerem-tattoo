How a piece is made

  1. Send a photo

    Your pet, an object, a moment. A short note on placement and size.

  2. Design

    I draw it for your body. You see the design before the day.

  3. Session

    Most pieces take one quiet sitting at Kerem Studio.

  4. Heal

    You leave with an aftercare guide. Fine line settles over a few weeks.

The hand behind the needle

I'm Kerem, a tattoo artist in Haifa. I work in micro realism: small, precise pieces, mostly portraits of pets, personal objects and quiet moments.

Everything happens at Kerem Studio, my own calm and clean space. Each tattoo is planned together with you, from the first idea to the stencil. About 12,700 people follow the work on Instagram.
