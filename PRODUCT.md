# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three confirmed audiences, all arriving on phones first:
1. Instagram followers of @kerem.g.tatt who tap the bio link. They already like the work; they need confidence and one obvious way to book.
2. New clients from Google searching "tattoo Haifa" or "pet tattoo Israel". They do not know Kerem; they need to be convinced from zero.
3. Tourists and visitors to Israel looking for an artist of international level. Hence English.

Job for all three: judge the work, trust the person, send one WhatsApp message with an idea.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio for Kerem Gavriel, tattoo artist in Haifa, Israel. It exists so the work is seen at a size Instagram never allows, and so booking is one tap. Success: a visitor sees 6–8 pieces within 10 seconds and taps the WhatsApp button.

## Positioning

Two truths a neighboring artist cannot copy:
- Micro-realism pet portraits: cats, dogs, huskies at needle-level detail. This is the recurring subject of her feed.
- Fine line that heals: precision aimed at lasting sharpness. (Healed photos are not yet on hand; this claim must be shown with real healed images before it is stated as a promise.)

## Operating Context

- Work happens at Kerem Studio, Haifa, her own private studio. Sponsored by Biotat (@biotat_).
- Booking is a WhatsApp conversation: idea, placement, rough size, then a proposal and date. No form, no email, no calendar tool.
- Instagram is the living feed; the site is the curated, large-format view.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English only (confirmed). WhatsApp is the only booking channel (confirmed). Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on Netlify (kerem-tattoo.netlify.app).
- Existing engines that may be kept or replaced at the design's discretion: spring lightbox (lightbox.js), magnifier "The Loupe" (effects.js).
- Undecided: pricing, deposit, reply time (owner has not supplied). Must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kerem Gavriel. Studio: Kerem Studio. Handle: @kerem.g.tatt.
- Theme: dark was chosen for v2; the owner did not pin it for the rebuild. Open.
- Tone: quiet, precise, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- 10 images scraped from Instagram at 640px max (img/tattoo-01..12, two duplicates dropped). Subjects: Persian cat, cat portrait, smiling dog, husky, heart-and-brain, child's house drawing, two vases, fish plate, mirror stencil, studio sign.
- Profile picture at 150px, upscaled to 640 (img/profile-hero.jpg). No editorial portrait.
- 12.7K Instagram followers (real, as of 2026-10-07).
- Absent, must not be fabricated: healed photos, testimonials, press, prices, studio interior photos, client before/after photos.
- Research on competitor sites: docs/research/2026-10-07-competitor-insights.md.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the argument. Images lead at the largest honest size; text is short.
2. One action everywhere: WhatsApp, never buried.
3. Nothing invented. Placeholders are hidden from visitors, listed for the owner.
4. Phone first. Instagram sends phones; desktop is the second viewport.
5. Quiet confidence. The artist is a person, not a studio brand.
