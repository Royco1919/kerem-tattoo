# Competitor research: micro-realism tattoo sites (2026-10-07)

Fetched + analysed 11 sites: dotstolines.com (Chaim Machlev), stephencarballo.com, hakanadik.com, ateliereva.com, dtrmodern.com/kozo (Eden Kozokaro), iglatattoo.com, gangatattoo.com, lisalanstudio.com, drwoo.com, balazsbercsenyi.com, ferunda.com.
Most star micro-realists (Ilya Brezinski, Mr.K, Sol, Oozy, Zihwa, Mira Mariah, JonBoy) have NO site — Instagram only. That is the gap Kerem's site fills.

Screenshots: ref-*.jpg in this folder.

## What the best share
1. Work first, lots of air. Short calm text.
2. Near-monochrome palette, at most one muted accent.
3. The artist is the brand: one editorial portrait (DotsToLines, Atelier Eva).
4. Booking rules stated up front: steps, minimum price, deposit, reschedule (Lisa Lan, Carballo, Hakan).
5. Booking button always in reach (nav + repeated).
6. Selective social proof: a few press logos, 2–3 reviews. No widgets.
7. Work curated into groups, not an endless feed.

## Common mistakes
Builder defaults (Wix blue, lorem meta), hero carousels, widget clutter, thumbnails too small to show fine detail, no captions (placement/size/healed), 17-section pages that repeat.

## World-class = 
Images ≥1200px with a detail crop; museum-style photo consistency; gallery-label captions (subject · placement · size · fresh/healed); scarcity + care tone (Dr. Woo); slow quiet motion.

## Direction for Kerem
Sections: Hero (one huge piece + wordmark + WhatsApp) → one-line statement → Selected work (filter: Pets / Portraits / Objects) → Pet portraits feature → Process (4 steps) → About (editorial B&W portrait, 12.7K quietly) → Healed work → FAQ/aftercare → Booking (WhatsApp intake, pre-filled) → Footer.
Fonts: display serif (Cormorant Garamond / Fraunces) + clean sans (Heebo / Inter). Palette (dark variant chosen by owner): ink #0b0b0b, bone #f2f0ec, graphite #8f8c86, hairline rgba(255,255,255,.1).
Signature ideas: (1) The Loupe — magnifier on hover/tap; (2) "Photo → skin" slider: client's pet photo vs tattoo; (3) Healed archive with dates.

## Do / Don't
Do: ≥1200px images + detail crop; captions; pre-filled WhatsApp; consistent photography; price/deposit up front.
Don't: hero carousel; IG feed / review widgets; script fonts, gold, grunge; builder defaults; let domain lapse.
